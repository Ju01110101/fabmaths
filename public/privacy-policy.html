FabMaths Privacy Policy

Last updated: 01/04/2026

FabMaths (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of parents, students and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you contact us, book a session, or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Parent details: name, email address, phone number
  • Student details: name, school year, exam board, learning needs
  • Booking information: preferred times, session history
  • Website data: form submissions, cookies, and basic analytics (for example, page views)

We only collect information that is relevant to providing tutoring services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Arrange and deliver tutoring sessions
  • Provide updates about lessons, homework or scheduling
  • Improve our services and communication
  • Meet legal or safeguarding obligations

We do not sell or share your data with third-party marketers.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

We process your information under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: to provide tutoring services you have requested
  • Legitimate interest: to communicate with you about your child’s progress
  • Consent: when you opt in to receive updates or marketing
  • Legal obligation: safeguarding or record-keeping requirements

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

We store your information securely using reputable, password-protected systems. We take reasonable steps to prevent loss, misuse or unauthorised access. We keep your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services or meet legal requirements.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share information only when necessary:

  • With tutors working under FabMaths
  • With safeguarding authorities if legally required

We do not share your data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request corrections
  • Request deletion
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Object to certain types of processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: fabrice@fab-maths.com

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
